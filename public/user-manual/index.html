01 System Overview

The Fleet Management System eliminates manual fleet billing. Employees raise a vehicle requisition in OneGP; once a vehicle is assigned and the trip is completed, the trip data flows into this system, which applies the configured billing rules and consolidates every trip for a vehicle into a single monthly invoice ready for review, approval and payment.

End-to-end business process

  1. Requisition raised in OneGP. An employee submits a vehicle requisition with pickup, destination and required time.
  2. Vehicle & driver assigned. Assignment is made on the basis of the employee's requirement and current vehicle availability.
  3. Trip data received. Requisition, assignment, employee and trip details reach the Fleet Management System over a secured REST/JSON API, with no or minimal user intervention.
  4. Trip executed. Start and end are recorded (in the system or from the driver's mobile in-app view), producing actual travel time and distance.
  5. Charges calculated. On trip completion the system applies the billing rules for that vehicle's category and fuel type.
  6. Monthly invoice consolidated. All trips for a vehicle in a billing month roll into one invoice that moves through Draft → Pending Approval → Approved → Paid.
  7. Status returned to OneGP. Invoice number, billing status, payment status and any error status are posted back to OneGP.

Two interfaces, one system

Web application (desktop)
  • Master data, rate cards, requisitions
  • Billing, invoice approval, adjustments
  • All eight reports with Excel export
  • Audit log, integration logs, user & role administration
Mobile in-app view
  • Drivers: their assigned trips only
  • Start Trip / End Trip from the phone
  • Route map, trip invoice view & PDF download
  • Staff: read-only vehicle list

02 Feature List

Everything the system does, grouped by module.

1. Master data management

  • Vehicle Master — vehicle number, category, fuel type, seat capacity, partner/vendor, monthly fixed rent, per-KM rate, OT rate per hour, plus recurring charges (personal usage bill, toll, parking, startup fuel, mobile bill, other charge) and status (Active / Inactive / Maintenance).
  • Driver Master — driver name, mobile number, driving licence number with attachment reference, NID number with attachment reference, vendor and status (Active / Inactive / Suspended).
  • Vehicle Categories — fully managed list (Sedan, SUV, Microbus 7-seater, Microbus 12-seater, Pickup, Minibus, Ambulance, Others, and any category you add).
  • Fuel Types — fully managed list (CNG, LPG, Octane, Diesel, Hybrid, and any type you add).
  • Rate Card — configurable monthly fixed rent, per-KM rate and OT rate per hour for every Vehicle Category × Fuel Type combination, with duplicate-combination prevention.
  • Create, edit and delete on every master list, with inline validation and duplicate checks.

2. Trip requisition management

  • Receives requisition and assignment data from OneGP: ticket ID, requestor ID, employee name, department / cost centre, request date & time, pickup location, destination, vehicle, category, driver, trip start & end, total travel time, total distance and trip status.
  • Manual requisition entry for trips raised outside OneGP, with map-based pickup and destination selection, address autocomplete and automatic route distance/duration.
  • Assign or reassign vehicle and driver, restricted to active records and blocked once a trip has started.
  • Start Trip / End Trip with automatic travel-time calculation and invoice generation on completion.
  • Extend Time with a mandatory reason, retaining a full extension history (who, when, previous and new end time, note).
  • Cancel or Reject a requisition so that no bill is produced.
  • Search across requisition ID, employee, department, vehicle, driver and vendor; filter by status, flagged or uninvoiced.
  • Route preview map, per-trip invoice preview and per-trip invoice PDF download.

3. Automated billing

  • Automatic bill calculation on trip completion using the vehicle's rates and the rate card for its category and fuel type.
  • Monthly consolidation: every completed trip for a vehicle in a billing month becomes one invoice.
  • Nine charge components on every invoice — fixed rent (body rent), personal usage bill, distance charge, overtime, toll, parking, startup fuel, mobile bill and other approved charges.
  • Invoice approval workflow: Draft → Pending Approval → Approved → Paid, with Reject and a rejection note.
  • Manual billing adjustment by authorised administrators, with a mandatory reason recorded in the audit log; negative amounts deduct.
  • Exception handling — no bill is generated for cancelled or rejected trips, invalid assignments or missing completion data.
  • Five exception flags surfaced on the requisition list for review: missing start/end time, missing distance, vehicle/driver mismatch, duplicate ticket ID, GPS data missing.
  • Printable invoice and PDF generation.

4. Reporting

  • Eight standard reports (see section 9): monthly vehicle-wise billing, vendor-wise billing, department-wise utilisation, distance travelled, overtime cost, category-wise cost analysis, fuel-type-wise cost analysis and driver utilisation.
  • Multi-select entity filter (vehicles, vendors, departments, drivers, categories, fuel types) plus month and year.
  • Excel export on every report, formatted with a report title row, bold headers, frozen header row and auto-sized columns.
  • Detailed 40-column vehicle billing export covering fuel-wise KM distribution, KM rates including tax, startup fuel, driver DA, toll/parking, extra service charge, mobile bill, adjustments, Iftar bill, VAT at 15% and grand total.
  • PDF output for invoices and trip invoices with page footers and generation timestamp.

5. Dashboard & analytics

  • Six KPI tiles: total vehicles, active drivers, trips in the month, pending invoices, amount billed and flagged trips.
  • Vehicle-category-wise billed amount bar chart.
  • Fleet composition by fuel type donut chart.
  • Recent trip requisitions table with a jump to the full list.

6. Security, audit & integration

  • Role-Based Access Control with configurable page-level permissions across 12 modules; menu items and pages a role is not granted are hidden and blocked.
  • Five seeded roles — Fleet Administrator, Billing Administrator, Finance, Approver, Read Only — plus unlimited custom roles.
  • User administration with role assignment, status control, password management and last-login visibility.
  • Secure session handling with HTTP-only cookies; inactive accounts are refused.
  • Audit log covering bill generation, bill approval, manual adjustments, master data changes, user activity and API transactions, filterable by module.
  • OneGP integration transaction log with inbound/outbound direction, payload type, reference ID, status and message for troubleshooting.

7. Mobile in-app view

  • Separate driver login by mobile number; staff login by email.
  • Driver home with trip counters by status; drivers see only their own trips.
  • Trip search and status filter chips; trip detail with route map.
  • Start Trip, End Trip, View Invoice and Download Invoice from the phone.
  • Profile with role, user ID and sign-out.

04 Signing In

  1. Open the application URL. You are taken to the sign-in screen.
  2. Enter your Email and Password. Use the eye icon to reveal the password if needed.
  3. Select Sign In. On success you land on the Dashboard.

Navigation after sign-in

  • Sidebar — grouped menu: Dashboard, Master Data, Trip Requisitions, Reports, Audit Log, Administration. Use the chevron in the sidebar header to collapse it to icons only; hover an icon to see its label. On a small screen use the hamburger icon in the top bar.

06 Master Data

Master data drives every calculation in the system. Set it up in this order: Vehicle Category → Fuel Type → Rate Card → Vehicle Master → Driver Master. Categories and fuel types populate the dropdowns used by rate cards and vehicles, so create them first.

6.1 Vehicle Master

Manage the vehicle registry, categories, fuel types and billing rates.

The list shows Vehicle, Category / Fuel / Seats, Partner, Rates (fixed rent, per KM, OT per hour), Charges (personal usage, toll, parking), Other Charges (startup fuel, mobile bill, other), Status and row actions.

Adding a vehicle

  1. Select Add Vehicle.
  2. Under Vehicle Details fill Vehicle Number (required), Vehicle Category, Fuel Type, Seat Capacity, Partner / Vendor (required) and Status — Active, Inactive or Maintenance.
  3. Under Billing Rates enter Monthly Fixed Rent, Per KM Rate and OT Rate / hr.
  4. Under Additional Charges enter the recurring monthly amounts billed alongside the fixed rent: Personal Usage Bill, Toll Charge, Parking Charge, Startup Fuel Charge, Mobile Bill and Other Charge.
  5. Select Save. A confirmation appears at the bottom of the screen.
Note

Only vehicles with status Active can be assigned to a trip. Set a vehicle to Maintenance to take it out of assignment without deleting its history.

Editing and deleting

Use the pencil icon to Edit a row and the bin icon to Delete it. Deletion is immediate and there is no confirmation step. Past trips and invoices keep the vehicle number they were billed against, so history is not lost — but the vehicle disappears from the assignment dropdowns and from future reports. Prefer setting a retired vehicle to Inactive.

6.2 Driver Master

Manage driver profiles, licence / NID documentation and vendor assignment.

  1. Select Add Driver.
  2. Enter Driver Name, Mobile Number, Driving License Number and NID Number — all required.
  3. Record the document references in License Attachment and NID Attachment.
  4. Enter the Vendor and set Status — Active, Inactive or Suspended.
  5. Select Save.
Tip

The mobile number is also the driver's identity in the mobile in-app view, so enter it accurately and keep the format consistent across the fleet.

6.3 Vehicle Categories & Fuel Types

Both are simple managed lists with the same behaviour: a counter chip, a single-field add/edit dialog, a duplicate-name check and row-level edit and delete. Whatever you maintain here appears in the Vehicle Master and Rate Card dropdowns.

Values in use cannot be deleted

If a category or fuel type is still referenced by a vehicle or a rate card, the system refuses the deletion and names what is still using it. Reassign or remove those records first. This protects the billing rates attached to the combination from being orphaned.

6.4 Rate Card Configuration

Configurable billing rates per Vehicle Category and Fuel Type combination — this is where the requirement “billing rates must be configurable for each Vehicle Category and Fuel Type combination” is satisfied.

  1. Select Add Rate Card.
  2. Choose the Vehicle Category and Fuel Type.
  3. Enter Monthly Fixed Rent (BDT), Per KM Rate (BDT) and OT Rate / hr (BDT).
  4. Select Save.
One card per combination

If a card already exists for that pair the system refuses it: “A rate card for this Category + Fuel Type combination already exists.” Edit the existing card instead. Aim for full coverage — one card for every combination your fleet actually uses.

07 Trip Requisitions

Trip data received from OneGP after vehicle requisition and assignment. This is the operational heart of the system — everything billed starts here.

Counters

  • Total Requisitions — every requisition in the system.
  • Invoiced Trips — completed trips matched to a monthly invoice.
  • Flagged for Review — requisitions with at least one exception flag.
  • Cancelled / Rejected — requisitions that will never be billed.

Finding a requisition

Type into Search by requisition id, employee, vehicle, driver... to match on requisition ID, employee, department, vehicle, driver or vendor. Narrow further with the Status filter: All, Completed, In Progress, Cancelled, Rejected, Flagged or Uninvoiced.

Creating a requisition manually

Requisitions normally arrive from OneGP. Use New Requisition for trips that need to be entered directly.

  1. Select New Requisition.
  2. Optionally pick a Vehicle (optional) and Driver (optional) — leave both blank to assign later from the list.
  3. Enter Employee Name and Department (both required) and the Vendor (optional).
  4. On the map, set the Pickup Location (green pin) and Destination (red pin). Search an address or use Click map to set: with Pickup / Destination, then drag the pins to fine-tune. The route and distance are calculated automatically.
  5. Set Approx. Start Time and Approx. End Time; the approximate duration is shown as a chip.
  6. Select Create Requisition. The requisition opens as In Progress.

Assigning a vehicle and driver

  1. Open the row menu and choose Assign Vehicle (or Reassign Vehicle / Assign Driver — all open the same dialog).
  2. Select the Vehicle. Only Active vehicles are listed; the card below confirms category, fuel type, seats, partner and per-KM rate.
  3. Select the Driver — Active drivers only, listed with their mobile number.
  4. Select Assign.
Assignment window

Vehicle and driver can only be assigned or reassigned while the trip is In Progress. Once the trip has started the assignment is locked so that billing always matches the vehicle that actually ran the trip.

Running a trip

ActionAvailable whenEffect
Start Trip Vehicle and driver assigned, trip not yet started, not cancelled or rejected. Records the actual start time and moves the trip to Started.
End Trip Trip started and not yet ended. Records the end time, computes total travel time, sets Completed and generates the bill.
Extend Time Trip started and not yet ended. Moves the planned end time with a mandatory reason, kept in the extension history.

View / View Invoice Always / when the trip is invoiced. Opens the requisition details, or the trip invoice preview.

Extending trip time

  1. Choose Extend Time from the row menu.
  2. Set the New Trip End Time.
  3. Enter the Reason for Extension (required) — the request is refused without it.
  4. Select Extend. The previous end time, the new end time, your name and the timestamp are stored and shown on the requisition details.

Requisition details

View opens the full record: requisition and employee information, assignment, the approximate window, the trip timeline (start, end, distance, travel time, invoice), any time extensions, any exception flags, and a route preview map. Footer buttons let you continue straight into Edit, Start Trip, End Trip or View Invoice.

Trip invoice

View Invoice shows the trip-level invoice for a completed trip: bill-to employee and department, trip date, vendor, driver, pickup, destination, vehicle and the distance charge line (distance × rate per km). Download PDF saves it. The footer states which monthly invoice the trip belongs to.

08 Billing & Invoices

Monthly vehicle invoices consolidated from approved trips, fixed rent, OT, toll, parking and other charges.

  • Total Invoiced, Paid and Pending / Draft summarise the value of the filtered set.
  • Use Billing Month to focus on one month or keep All Months.
  • The grid lists Invoice Number, Vehicle, Partner / Vendor, Billing Month, Trips, Total Bill and Status. Select an invoice number to open it.
  • Generate Monthly Bills triggers billing for completed, unbilled trips.
How invoices come into existence

An invoice is created automatically when a trip is ended — the confirmation reads “Trip ended. Invoice generated.” Further completed trips for the same vehicle in the same month are consolidated into that single monthly invoice. Generate Monthly Bills is the manual re-trigger for a period close.

8.1 Billing logic

Total Bill = Monthly Fixed Rent (Body Rent)
  + Personal Usage Bill
  + Distance Charge  (Distance KM × Per KM Rate)
  + Overtime Charge  (OT hours × OT rate per hour)
  + Toll Charge
  + Parking Charge
  + Startup Fuel Charge
  + Mobile Bill
  + Other Approved Charges

Rules applied

  • Bills are generated automatically after trip completion.
  • KM rates are taken from the Vehicle Category and Fuel Type combination on the rate card, with the vehicle's own rate as the fallback.
  • Overtime is charged when trip duration exceeds the configured working hours.
  • Monthly fixed rent is allocated according to the configured business rule.
  • Multiple trips for the same vehicle within a billing month consolidate into one monthly invoice.
  • All billing parameters — KM rate, OT rate, fixed rent and the recurring charges — are maintained through the master tables, never hard-coded.

When no bill is generated

  • The trip was cancelled before starting.
  • The trip was rejected.
  • The vehicle assignment is invalid.
  • Trip completion information is missing.

Trips in these states are excluded from billing but remain visible and auditable. See Appendix B for the flags raised when trip data looks incomplete.

8.2 Invoice detail & approval

The Charge Breakdown card itemises all nine components and the total:

  1. Monthly Fixed Vehicle Rent (Body Rent)
  2. Personal Usage Bill
  3. Distance Charge — shown as distance km × rate
  4. Overtime Charge — shown with the OT hours
  5. Toll Charge
  6. Parking Charge
  7. Startup Fuel Charge
  8. Mobile Bill
  9. Other Approved Charges

Invoice Details shows vehicle category, partner / vendor, trip count, generated date, approved by and approved date. Any manual adjustment note is displayed as a highlighted alert.

8.3 Manual billing adjustment

Authorised Fleet Administrators can correct an invoice without editing the underlying trip data. All manual adjustments are recorded in the audit log with mandatory notes.

  1. Open the invoice and select Manual Adjustment.
  2. Enter the Adjustment Amount (BDT, use negative to deduct). A positive value increases the bill; a negative value reduces it.
  3. Enter the Reason / Note (required).
  4. Select Apply Adjustment — enabled only when a reason is present and the amount is not zero.
Audit obligation

Write the reason so that a reviewer months later can understand it without asking you — reference the ticket ID, the log book, or the vendor communication that justifies the change.

Running a report

  1. Open the report from the Reports hub or the sidebar.

  4. Select Search. Until you do, the page shows the prompt to select filters — this is expected, not an error.

The detailed vehicle billing export

The on-screen Vehicle Billing Report is a summary; the Excel export is the full billing sheet used for vendor settlement — around 40 columns under grouped headers, with the header rows frozen:

  • Identification — SL No., vehicle no., mobile no., vehicle type, rent basis (monthly / daily).
  • Vehicle usage duration — from and to.
  • Fuel consumption reference rate and total KM run as per log book.
  • Fuel-wise KM distribution and KM rate including tax, per fuel (Octane, LPG, CNG, Hybrid).
  • Total KM cost per fuel plus the total.
  • Start-up fuel cost with tax; driver DA with tax (days and amount).
  • Toll and parking with tax; rent amount with tax.
  • Extra service charge with tax — rate, hours, amount.
  • Mobile bill; adjustment / absent; Iftar bill rate, number of days and amount.
  • Total amount, Add 15% VAT, total amount with VAT & tax, and remarks.
Tip

Adjustments and absences must be entered as negative amounts to deduct — the column is summed into the total, not subtracted from it.

10 Audit Log

Audit trail for bill generation, approvals, manual adjustments, master data changes, user activity and API transactions.

  • Columns: Timestamp, User, Action, Module, Details.
  • Filter with Module — keep All Modules or pick one (Billing, Vehicle Master, Driver Master, Rate Card, User Management, OneGP Integration and others).
  • Refresh pulls the latest entries.
  • Sort or filter any column from its header menu.
Read-only by design

Audit entries cannot be edited or deleted from the application. This is what makes the log usable as evidence during an audit.

11 OneGP Integration

API-based REST/JSON transaction logs between OneGP and the Fleet Management System.

What flows where

OneGP → Fleet Management (inbound)
  • Vehicle requisition
  • Vehicle assignment
  • Employee information
  • Trip details
  • Trip completion status
Fleet Management → OneGP (outbound)
  • Invoice number
  • Billing status
  • Payment status
  • Error status
  • Three counters: Inbound (OneGP → Fleet), Outbound (Fleet → OneGP) and Failed / Retried.
  • Columns: Timestamp, Direction, Payload Type, Reference ID, Status, Message.
  • Use the reference ID to trace a specific requisition or invoice end to end across both systems.
When a trip has not arrived

If a requisition is missing from the Fleet Management System, check this log for a failed inbound transaction carrying that ticket ID and read the message before raising it with the OneGP team.

12 Users & Roles

Users

Manage application users and assign each one a role. Configure what a role can access on the Roles page.

  1. Select Add User.
  2. Enter the Full Name and Email — the email is the sign-in identity.
  3. Set the Password. When editing an existing user the field reads New Password (leave blank to keep current).
  4. Choose the Role — required; it determines what the user can see.
  5. Set Status to Active or Inactive, then Save.
Deactivate rather than delete

Setting a leaver to Inactive blocks sign-in immediately while preserving their name on the audit entries and approvals they made.

Roles

Define roles and the menus / pages each role is permitted to access.

  1. Select Add Role.
  2. Enter the Role Name (required) and a Description.
  3. Set Status.
  4. Under Menu & Page Permissions, tick the modules the role may open. They are grouped as General, Master Data and Administration.
  5. Select Save. Users holding the role pick up the change on their next page load.
Roles in use cannot be deleted

A role that is still assigned to at least one user cannot be removed — the system refuses it and tells you to reassign those users first. This prevents a user being left without any permissions.

Separation of duties

Grant Billing & Invoices plus Reports to Finance for approval and payment, and keep Users, Roles and Integration Logs with the Fleet Administrator only. Nobody outside the fleet team needs master data write access.

13 Mobile In-App View

A phone-sized interface for drivers and field staff, reached at /in-app-view. Drivers see only the trips assigned to them; staff additionally get a read-only vehicle list.

Signing in

  1. Open the in-app view on the phone.
  2. Choose Driver Login or Staff Login.
  3. Drivers: enter your Mobile Number and password — your password is your mobile number. Staff: enter your email and password.
  4. Select Sign In.
Inactive driver

“This driver account is inactive. Contact your Fleet Administrator.” means the driver's status in the Driver Master is not Active.

Home

A greeting with your role, then tappable counters — My Trips (drivers) or Total Requisitions (staff), Trips In Progress, Started Trips, Completed Trips and Vehicles for staff. Tapping a counter opens the trip list already filtered. The bottom bar carries Home, Trips, Vehicles (staff) and Profile.

Trips

Search on ticket, employee, route or vehicle, and filter with the chips All, In Progress, Started, Completed, Cancelled, Rejected. Each card shows the ticket, status, employee and department, the pickup → destination route, the vehicle and the request time. Tap a card for the details.

Trip details and trip actions

The detail screen shows the route on a map plus employee, department, vehicle, driver, vendor, the approximate window, the actual start and end, and whether the trip is invoiced. The action buttons are:

  • Start Trip — when a vehicle and driver are assigned and the trip has not started.
  • End Trip — when the trip is running; this completes the trip and generates the bill.
  • View Invoice — opens the trip invoice.
  • Download Invoice — saves the trip invoice PDF.

Profile

Your name, sign-in identity, Role and User ID, with Sign Out.

Driver routine

Open the trip, tap Start Trip when you pick the employee up, and End Trip as soon as you drop them off. Accurate start and end times are what make the distance charge and overtime correct — and prevent the trip from being flagged.

B Exception Flags

The system flags trip data that looks incomplete or inconsistent so it can be corrected before billing. Flagged requisitions are counted on the dashboard and on the requisitions page, and can be isolated with the Flagged status filter.

FlagRaised whenWhat to do
Missing Start/End Time The trip has no recorded actual start or end. Confirm the times with the driver or log book and record them before billing.
Missing Distance No distance came through for a completed trip. Check the GPS feed and the integration log; correct against the log book.
Vehicle/Driver Mismatch The vehicle or driver on the trip differs from the assignment. Establish which actually ran the trip and reconcile before approving.
Duplicate Ticket ID The same OneGP ticket arrived more than once. Keep one record and cancel the duplicate so the trip is not billed twice.
GPS Data Missing No GPS / trip telemetry for the trip. Verify with the GPS provider; fall back to the log book distance and note the source.
Monthly close routine

Before generating monthly bills: filter requisitions to Flagged and clear every item, then filter to Uninvoiced to confirm no completed trip has been missed.
